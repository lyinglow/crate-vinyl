# How Crate compares to other vinyl apps

Checked on 11 November 2026 from App Store listings, review sites and Discogs help pages. Listings are marketing, and paid features inside apps could not be seen. Treat this as a first look, not proof.

## Already done by others

| Feature | Who has it |
|---|---|
| Cover flow browsing | [My Vinyl+](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1547173908), [Groupies](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6740731188) (background colours follow the artwork), [Color Flow](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6739723075) |
| Digging by genre, year and format, with YouTube previews and a wantlist that syncs to Discogs | [Diggin' It](https://mwm.ai/apps/diggin-it/6759758090) |
| Wantlist with two way Discogs sync | [MOVINYL](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6751862867), My Vinyl+ |
| Collection stats by genre and decade | [Vinyl Catalog](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vinyl-catalog/id6759412038), [Groovv](https://alternativeto.net/software/groovv/about/) |
| Sort by most collected and most wanted, with style and year filters | The [Discogs website](https://support.discogs.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003622014-How-To-Browse-Search-In-The-Database). It cannot hide what you already own. |

## Not found in the same search

- A private Spotify playlist of every track of every vinyl you add, and one for hearted tracks. [VinylCrate](https://mwm.ai/apps/vinyl-crate/6754758604) goes the other way, matching an Apple Music playlist to records. [Kallax](https://apps.apple.com/app/kallax/id6758233437) makes DJ playlists by tempo with no Spotify link.
- Turning a sleeve over to its back and label photos inside the cover flow.
- Dig's Heard marks, and a Not interested list you can bring records back from.
- Free, no subscription, records kept on the phone.

## What to claim

No single feature is unique. The honest line is one free app that joins the collection, Spotify, the wanted list and digging.

## What could widen the gap

- Similar tracks by decade, genre, tempo and feel, joined with Dig (see ideas.md).
- "Do I own this?" mode for shopping.
- Price alerts on wanted records.
