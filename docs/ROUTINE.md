# A routine for 2,000 records

Aim: about 6 sittings of 20 minutes each, spread over a couple of weeks. Not one long push.

## Before you start (one time, 15 minutes)

1. Set up Crate (see SETUP.md).
2. Already have a Discogs collection? Import it first. Everything in it appears at once. You only scan what is missing.
3. Set your usual grade in **Settings**, for example VG+ for both. Each record then starts with it. You only change the exceptions.
4. Finish your Discogs seller settings, so prices work.

## Sort the pile first

Do this once. It saves more time than anything else.

- **Pile A, has a barcode.** Newer records and reissues. About 70% of most collections.
- **Pile B, no barcode.** Older records. These go through Search by catalogue number.
- **Pile C, odd ones.** White labels, bootlegs, test pressings. These are done by hand, last.

## A sitting

1. Plug in your phone. Keep the screen on. Prices load in the background while you scan.
2. Open **Scan**. Set the phone against a stand or lean it on a shelf, so one hand is free.
3. For each record in pile A:
   - Hold the barcode inside the box. It beeps.
   - Check the sleeve on screen matches the one in your hand.
   - Tap **Add to library**. The camera is ready for the next one.
   - Only if different: tap **Other matches** or **Other pressings**, or change a grade.
4. Expect 6 to 10 records a minute on pile A. A sitting of 20 minutes gives 120 to 200.
5. Stop when your attention drops. Matching the wrong pressing is the common error when tired.

## Pile B (no barcode)

1. Open **Scan**, then **Search**.
2. Type the catalogue number from the spine or label. The first few characters are often enough.
3. Tap the result with the right country and year. Compare the label photo if unsure.
4. Expect 2 to 4 records a minute.

## Pile C (odd ones)

Use **By hand**. Artist and title are the only must-haves. Add a photo of the sleeve and the label. Later, open the record and tap **Match on Discogs** if you find it.

## The weekly tidy (5 minutes)

1. **Settings, Back up everything.**
2. Open **Library**, tap the filter button, and look for records with no grade. Fix them in a batch.
3. Check the sync bar at the top is not showing an error.

## When all 2,000 are in

1. **Value** tab. Look at the total and the most valuable records.
2. **Worth selling.** Go down the list. Tap **Keep** on records you will never sell. Tap **Sell** on ones you could let go.
3. In the sell sheet, check the suggested price for your grade and the cheapest copy for sale now. Open the Discogs page if you want to see last sold, median and high.
4. Tap **Save as a draft on Discogs** for your first few. Check them on Discogs. Then use **List on Discogs** for the rest.
5. Every few days, **Selling, Check Discogs for sales**. Sold records move over automatically. Crate offers to remove them from your Discogs collection.
