# Crate

Your vinyl collection on your iPhone, linked to Discogs.

- The app is this folder (index.html, js, css). Connect the repo to Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages and it deploys as it is. No build step.
- `docs/PLAN.md` is the plan, the honest answer on scanning, and the rate limits.
- `docs/SETUP.md` is step by step setup.
- `docs/ROUTINE.md` is a routine for working through 2,000 records.
